Privacy Policy

Tafseer ul Quran al Kareem (Hafiz Abdul Salam bin Muhammad Bhutvi, Rahimahullah) · Effective 6 November 2026

In short: the app is free, has no ads and no user accounts, and we never sell your data. Your bookmarks, reading progress, settings and tasbih counts stay on your phone. We use Google Firebase only to understand app usage, fix crashes and send notifications.

1. Who we are

Tafseer ul Quran al Kareem is a free, non-profit app that presents the Urdu translation and tafseer of Hafiz Abdul Salam bin Muhammad Bhutvi (Rahimahullah). It is developed by Naeem ur Rehman with a small team of volunteers. In this policy "we" means that team.

2. Information that stays on your device

The following is saved only on your phone and is never sent to us:

  • Bookmarks, last read page and reading position
  • Reading settings such as text size, layout and theme
  • Tasbih counts and any custom dhikr you add
  • Downloaded Quran fonts and audio files

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this.

3. Location (Qibla only)

If you open the Qibla tab, the app asks for your location to work out the Qibla direction. The direction is calculated on your phone. To show your city name, the coordinates are passed to your phone's built-in geocoding service (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android). We do not store or receive your location, and the app does not use location in the background. You can refuse or withdraw the permission in your phone's settings at any time; only the Qibla tab is affected.

4. Information collected by services we use

ServiceWhyWhat it may collect
Firebase Analytics (Google)To see which features are used, in aggregate App usage events, device model, OS version, approximate country, an app instance ID and, on Android, the advertising ID
Firebase Crashlytics (Google)To find and fix crashes Crash reports, device model, OS version, app version

We do not collect your name, phone number, email or contacts, and we do not use any of this information for advertising. Google's handling of this data is described in the Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Downloads and audio

When you download Quran fonts or listen to recitations, files are fetched from content servers (Cloudflare, mp3quran.net, Mawaqit's CDN and similar). Like any website, these servers see your IP address to deliver the file. No other information is sent.

6. Photos

If you choose to save an ayah as an image, the app asks for permission to add it to your photo library. It only writes the image you saved; it does not read your photos.

7. Links to other sites

The app links to YouTube, Facebook, WhatsApp, Telegram and other sites. Those services have their own privacy policies, which apply once you leave the app.

8. Children

The app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Your choices

  • Deny or withdraw location, notification and photo permissions in your phone's settings.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Google settings; on iPhone the app does not request tracking permission.
  • Contact us to ask any question about your data.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
